Use the tokens {patient} and {hospital} anywhere in your script and they'll be replaced live in the preview. The hospital name also appears as the message sender at the top of the thread.

Template format

Import a spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv) with these columns:
Step ID · Bot Message · Choice Text · Choice Next
Rows sharing a Step ID are grouped: each Bot Message becomes a texted bubble; the Choice Text is the patient's scripted reply, and Choice Next is the Step ID it advances to.
