## Maria Lopez — case detail (talk track)

**Why she’s in the queue:** Week-4 automated check-in captured **clinical concern + missed program touch + explicit callback request** without anyone cold-calling a list.

**What staff sees:**

- Program: Beyond Health GLP-1 Continuity  
- Encounter / enrollment: Weight Management – Week 4  
- Responses: Med adherence = Yes · Side effects = Yes (nausea) · Nutrition visit = Missed · Callback = Yes  
- Open issues: Side Effect · Missed Visit · Callback Requested  
- SLA: contact same business day  

**Close-the-loop actions (demo):**

1. Call Maria — acknowledge nausea, triage per clinic protocol.  
2. Reschedule nutrition visit.  
3. Document resolution on issue panel; close or escalate.  
4. Optional: note that the same dashboard would show James (Rx access) and Denise (silent) — *visibility into where patients drop off*.
